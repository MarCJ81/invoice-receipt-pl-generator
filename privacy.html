Privacy policy

Invoice, Receipt & P&L Generator · last updated 19 November 2026

The short version: everything you type stays in your browser. There is no account, no upload and no server behind this page that could receive your documents.

Nothing is uploaded

This tool is a single page that runs entirely in your browser. The details you enter, the line items, the amounts and the logo you choose are read with JavaScript on your own computer and are never sent anywhere.

  • The logo image is read with the browser's FileReader and kept in memory as a data URL for the preview and the printout.
  • All totals, tax, discount and profit figures are computed in the page itself, in integer cents. Nothing is logged.
  • A JSON or CSV export is created by your browser and written to your own Downloads folder.
  • The page keeps working with no network connection once it has loaded.

Draft stored in your browser

If you press Save draft, the current form is written to your browser's localStorage under the key invoice-pl:draft:v1, so it is still there when you come back. That copy never leaves your device, and Clear removes it. Your theme choice is stored under invoice-pl:theme in the same way. You can delete both by clearing site data in your browser settings.

Advertising and cookies

This tool does not currently display advertising. If advertising is added in the future, third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on a visit to this site or other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Any such change will be described on this page before it takes effect.

Because the contents of a document never leave the page, no advertising or analytics system on this site can see what you typed into it.

Children

This tool is intended for business use by adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Since no personal data is collected or stored by this site, there is no stored record to access, correct or delete. Any draft in localStorage is under your control and can be removed from your browser at any time.
